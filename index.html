English Script

SETTING: England, London, AUGUST 17 1980 MY APARTMENT – LATE MORNING 11:30 am **RINGGGGG.** **The cries of the telephone rang through the quiet hallway. I sat right beside it, my broad shoulders tense, my bright, restless eyes fixed on the phone.** ME (DAVID) Hello, who is this? VOICE ON PHONE (MARCUS) DAVID, we need you here at the station ASAP! We finally got a case! **I jolted upright, the words still ringing in my head, my heart beating out of my chest as I scrambled for my brand new clothes. I grabbed my coat and sprinted out of my apartment.** SETTING: CITY STREETS – LATE MORNING (11:45 am) **The city looked as it always did, breathtaking. The cobblestone street was lined with modest houses. The yelling of vendors usually annoyed me, but today it was music to my ears.** SETTING: POLICE STATION – MIDDAY (12:00 PM) ME (DAVID) BANG! (BURST THROUGH THE DOOR) A CASE? WE HAVE A CASE? **A weeping wrinkly old granny greeted my eyes. To her left stood James Maddison, a short, bald, rat-like man who was a journalist by day and a detective by night. In the corner, Marcus Bartty, a well-built, blond man, watched me.** MARCUS (running through the case) Ah, there you are! We have quite an important case here, David. Jane, you know, the mayor's daughter, has gone missing, and this granny here was the last person to see her. **The granny looked about fifty, her silver hair pulled neatly into a bun. Marcus and I questioned her while James scribbled key information in his notepad.** MARCUS First of all, what is your affiliation with Ms. Jane? GRANNY Why, I am her caretaker, I've been taking care of her ever since she was a tiny tot (wipe away tears) (ME) DAVID Last time you saw her? GRANNY Early morning, around quarter past 7, I would say. I saw her frolicking around in the garden shortly before she disappeared. BUT I did see a rather mysterious, short man by the fences before I left Jane alone to prepare breakfast. **Marcus pressed her for information about the man** GRANNY I am truly sorry, dear, I couldn't make out the man's appearance. ME (DAVID) It's fine, ma'am. Could the three of us scout the area tomorrow to find clues that would lead us to answers? GRANNY Of course, dear. All I want is that we find Ms. Jane as soon as possible. SETTING: CRIME SCENE – NEXT DAY AFTERNOON (1:00 PM) **Later in the afternoon, we headed towards the crime scene. We strutted along the street. The house stood out like white in a pool of black.** (ME) DAVID Wow this house is perfect; it isn't too big nor too small. Its architectural integrity was unlike anything I had ever seen in history. I'd fancy a house like this, wouldn't you, James? (AWESTRUCK) JAMES huh? Oh, yes, for sure (SCARDY, ANXIOUS) MARCUS You're rather quiet today. Now that I think about it, you were pretty quiet yesterday, too. (IN A JOLLY WAY) HOARSE VOICE (MIDDLE-AGED MAN) Are you guys here for the girl? **A middle-aged man appeared behind us. He quickly explained that he was Jane's neighbor.** MARCUS Yes, do you know anything about her disappearance? MIDDLE-AGED MAN (LEANS IN) I saw a creepy, bald guy over there by the bushes (POINTS AT BUSHES) (LOOKS AT JAMES) Hey, the guy looks a tad bit like JAMES THAT'S VERY VALUABLE INFORMATION, SIR (SHOUTING OVER MIDDLE-AGED MAN) (PRETENDING TO SCRIBBLE DOWN NOTES) Now, don't we have to get on with the case? (LEADS MARCUS AND DAVID AWAY FROM THE MAN TOWARDS THE HOUSE) MARCUS (KNOCKS ON DOOR) GRANNY Why, hello dears (HAS TISSUES, WIPING OFF TEAR STILL) MARCUS May we scout the premises? GRANNY Yes, oh please do your best to find my sweet baby (PLEADING) (GROUP TRY FINDING CLUES) (JAMES DOES NOT SEEM TO BE HELPING WITH THE CASE) **It had been hours, yet none of us found anything that could lead us to the perpetrator…** ME (DAVID) (SCRUFFLING THROUGH BUSHES) What's this? (PICKS UP POCKET WATCH) **I picked up a gold, scratched pocket watch by the bushes.** ME (DAVID) Ooo, it's pretty cold, must've been here for a day **I turned it over Two words in bold, fancy letter:** ME (DAVID) James Maddison.

Script as a Narrative

RINGGGGG The cries of the telephone rang through the quiet hallway. Right beside the phone sat a tall, red-headed man with broad shoulders and bright, restless eyes. "Hello, who is this?" said the man in a lively voice "DAVID, we need you here at the station ASAP! We finally got a case!" David jolted upright, the words still ringing through his head, his heart beating out of his chest as he scrambled for his brand new clothes. He took his coat and sprinted out of his apartment towards the police station. The city looked as it always did, breathtaking. The cobblestone street was lined with multiple modest houses. The yelling of vendors usually annoyed David, but today they were music to his ears. He finally reached the station. BANG. He burst through the door. A WEEPING, wringly old granny greeted David's eyes. To the granny's left stood James Maddison, a short, bald, rat-like man who was a journalist in the day and a detective at night. At the corner of the room stood Marcus Bartty, a well-built, blond man. Marcus quickly ran through the case with David. The mayor's 7-year-old daughter had disappeared earlier this morning, and the granny in front of them was the last person to see her. The granny looked about 50. She had her silver hair pulled neatly into a bun. Marcus and David questioned the old lady while James jotted down key information in his notepad. The granny was Jane's (The mayor's daughter) caretaker, and she had seen Jane frolicking around in the garden shortly before she disappeared. "Oh dear, I forgot to mention that I saw a rather short man by the fences before I left Jane alone to prepare breakfast," said the granny Marcus pressed her for information about the man, but it came to no avail. David asked her if the three of them could scout the area to find clues that would lead them to answers. "Of course, dear, all I want is that we find Jane as soon as possible," said the granny The next day, later on in the afternoon, the group headed towards the crime scene. They strutted along the street. The house stood out like white in a pool of black. The house was perfect; it wasn't too big nor was it too small. The architectural integrity of the house was unlike anything ever seen in history, but the garden was a humble size; it had small white fences surrounding it. "Are you guys here for the girl?" said a hoarse voice A middle-aged man appeared behind them. He quickly explained that he was Jane's neighbor and that he saw a bald man standing by the bushes this morning. Unexpectedly, James shooed him off, and the group made their way to the front door. Marcus asked for permission from the mayor to scout the premises. It had been hours, yet none of them found anything that could lead them towards the perpetrator, until... David had spotted a gold, scratched pocket watch by the bushes. He held the watch. It felt cold. He turned the watch over. It had 2 words engraved in fancy, bold letters. "James Maddison"
